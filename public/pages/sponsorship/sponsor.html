How sponsorship works on Spopeer

This page is open to every user type. Discovery happens here, but trust-building and negotiation should continue in a protected sponsor conversation after an offer is accepted or an invitation is sent.

Athletes, coaches and clubs can search active sponsor offers by sport, region and support type.
Anyone seeking support can publish what they need for games, tournaments, travel, kits, facilities or long-term development.
Supportive professionals and brands can publish sponsor offers and review sponsorship requests from verified users.
Accepted matches should open a separate sponsor chat for contracts, deliverables, proof of payment and updates.
