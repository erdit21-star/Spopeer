Common Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about Spopeer's platform, features, and how to get started.

Spopeer is a global sports networking platform that connects athletes, coaches, clubs, and sports professionals to collaborate, grow, and build their futures in sports. Our platform uses AI matching, a sports marketplace, and blockchain security to empower every user.

Yes! Spopeer is open to athletes, coaches, clubs, scouts, physiotherapists, and all other sports professionals. Whether you're an amateur or professional, or play a mainstream or niche sport, there's a place for you on Spopeer.

Spopeer is free to join and use for basic features. We offer premium features and services for users looking to unlock advanced tools and opportunities to grow their sports careers and businesses.

Athletes can showcase their skills, connect with coaches and clubs, access training programs, track performance analytics, build sponsorship opportunities, and monetize their talent through various platform features like the sports marketplace and stock exchange.

Coaches can discover promising athletes, build their brand, connect with clubs and athletes globally, create and sell training programs, and earn new revenue streams by sharing their expertise with a worldwide audience.

Clubs can recruit exceptional talent, build engaged fan communities, manage digital assets on blockchain, trade player tokens, and connect with coaches and sponsors globally to expand their reach and revenue streams.

Sports professionals like physiotherapists, analysts, scouts, and managers can expand their professional networks, connect with global opportunities, and scale their services across 1,500+ sports categories from 200+ countries.

Yes! Spopeer's AI matchmaking system helps connect athletes and clubs with potential sponsors. Additionally, our Sports Stock Exchange allows investors to fund athletes and clubs through blockchain-powered tokens.

Spopeer uniquely combines a comprehensive sports network, AI-powered matchmaking, a sports marketplace, blockchain security, sports stock exchange, and specialized tools all in one platform. We're dedicated to empowering sports professionals worldwide.

We use blockchain verification, profile authentication, and community reviews to maintain trust and credibility on the platform. All transactions and user data are encrypted for maximum security.

Users can share performance videos, training content, achievements, comments, and collaborate with other professionals. Our platform supports media uploads, career milestones, and community engagement.

Yes! Spopeer supports live streaming features for events, training sessions, and matches. This allows athletes and coaches to connect with audiences in real-time and monetize their content.

The Spopeer Marketplace is a one-stop platform where users can buy and sell sports gear, training programs, coaching services, and other sports-related products and services from verified professionals worldwide.

We follow the latest security standards and regulations, using encryption, blockchain verification, and secure payment processing to protect all user data. Your privacy is our priority.
