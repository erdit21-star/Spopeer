Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a Spopeer account?

Click the "Sign Up" button at the top of the page, select your user type (Athlete, Coach, Club, or Professional), and fill in your information. You'll need to verify your email before your account is fully active.

How do I find other athletes or coaches?

Use the Search feature to browse by sport, location, and user type. You can also explore featured profiles and join communities related to your interests.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and privacy controls. You can manage what information is visible to others in your privacy settings. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

How do I report inappropriate behavior?

If you encounter any inappropriate behavior or content, click the "Report" button on their profile or visit our Report Abuse page. Our moderation team will review your report promptly.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, you can delete your account anytime from your account settings. Please note that deleting your account will remove all your profile information and messages.

How do I update my profile information?

Log in to your account, go to your profile, and click "Edit Profile." You can update your bio, photo, skills, and other information. Remember to save your changes.

Is there a mobile app?

Spopeer is currently available as a web platform optimized for mobile browsers. Native mobile apps are in development.

How do I contact support?

If you can't find the answer you're looking for, visit our Contact Us page to send us a message directly.

Didn't find what you're looking for?

Our support team is here to help! Reach out to us directly.
