Report Abuse

At Spopeer, we're committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and inclusive community for all sports enthusiasts. If you encounter any content or behavior that violates our community standards, please report it using this form. Your report helps us maintain the integrity of our platform.

Important: All reports are treated confidentially and thoroughly investigated. We take abuse seriously and will take appropriate action based on our findings.

What Happens After You Report?

1. Acknowledgment: We'll confirm receipt of your report within 24 hours.

2. Investigation: Our moderation team will thoroughly investigate your claim.

3. Action: Depending on our findings, we may issue warnings, suspend, or permanently remove accounts that violate our community standards.

4. Follow-up: We'll notify you of any actions taken (while respecting user privacy).

Your Safety: If you're in immediate danger, please contact local law enforcement. Spopeer supports a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and abuse.

Community Guidelines

By reporting abuse, you help us maintain the Spopeer community as a safe space for athletes, coaches, clubs, and sports professionals. For more information about our community standards, please review our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
