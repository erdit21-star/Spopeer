Community Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-06-09

1. Respect others

Harassment, hate speech, threats, and abuse are not allowed.

2. Keep it authentic

Do not impersonate others or misrepresent achievements, credentials, or affiliations.

3. No spam or scams

Unsolicited promotion, scam links, and repeated disruptive posting are prohibited.

4. Protect privacy and safety

Do not post sensitive personal information about yourself or others.

5. Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, content removal, temporary suspension, or account bans.
