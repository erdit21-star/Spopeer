1. Introduction

Welcome to Spopeer, the global sports networking platform operated by Sports For All. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information in accordance with international data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar privacy laws.

We believe privacy is a fundamental right. This policy is designed to be transparent about our practices while empowering you with control over your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

We collect and retain the following personal information as long as you maintain an account with Spopeer:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, password, phone number, date of birth, and profile picture
  • Profile Data: Full name, location, country of residence, bio, sports interests, achievements, certifications, and other profile details
  • Contact Information: Mailing address, email address, phone number, and communication preferences
  • Transaction History: Payment information (processed securely through third-party payment processors), purchase history, and financial records
  • Communication Records: Messages, chat history, support tickets, and feedback submitted to our team
  • Voluntarily Submitted Information: Content you create, upload, or share on Spopeer, including posts, videos, photos, and articles

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, device identifiers, and mobile network information
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, interactions with features, search queries, and clicks
  • Location Data: Approximate location derived from IP address; precise location only with your explicit consent
  • Analytical Data: Web traffic patterns, user behavior analytics, and engagement metrics to improve our services
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: Details explained in our Tracking Technologies section

Information from Third Parties

  • Social Media: If you link your Spopeer account to social media profiles, we may receive limited profile information with your consent
  • Service Providers: Payment processors and analytics providers may share aggregated data
  • Other Users: When other users mention you, tag you in content, or provide information about you

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide, maintain, and improve Spopeer, while respecting your privacy:

Primary Uses

  • Service Delivery: Creating and managing your account, authenticating login, and providing platform features
  • Personalization: Customizing your experience, recommending relevant connections, content, and opportunities
  • Communication: Sending service updates, security alerts, policy changes, and support responses
  • Promotional Activities: Newsletters, promotional campaigns, and announcements (only with your opt-in consent)
  • Location-Based Services: Offering geographically relevant opportunities, events, and content
  • Analytics & Improvement: Understanding usage patterns, improving performance, and developing new features
  • Feedback & Support: Addressing your inquiries, resolving issues, and improving customer service

Legal & Safety Uses

  • Enforcing our Terms of Service and other agreements
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Complying with legal obligations and government requests
  • Protecting the rights, privacy, safety, and property of Spopeer, our users, and the public

🔐 Data Minimization:

  • We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our services
  • We do NOT post on your behalf to third-party social media without explicit consent
  • We do NOT sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

Under GDPR and similar regulations, we process your personal data based on the following lawful grounds:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing required to provide services you've requested
  • Legitimate Interests: Protecting our platform, detecting fraud, and improving services
  • Consent: For promotional communications, marketing, and optional features (you can withdraw consent anytime)
  • Legal Obligation: Complying with laws, regulations, and government requests
  • Vital Interests: Protecting your safety and security

We regularly review our processing activities to ensure they align with data protection principles and your interests.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We do NOT share your personal information with third-party marketers or advertisers
  • ❌ We do NOT sell your personal data
  • ❌ We do NOT post on your behalf without explicit consent

Limited Sharing

We only share your information with trusted partners in the following scenarios:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors, cloud hosting, email providers, and analytics tools (bound by data protection agreements)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request
  • Platform Operations: Other users may see your public profile information (name, profile picture, sports interests)
  • Blockchain Features (Optional): If you participate in Sports Stock Exchange, limited transaction data is recorded on blockchain (immutable and transparent)

Your Control

You control what information is public vs. private through your privacy settings. You can:

  • Choose who can see your profile (public, friends only, private)
  • Control visibility of your posts, achievements, and activities
  • Opt-out of promotional communications at any time

6. Tracking Technologies

We use tracking technologies to enhance your experience and understand how you use Spopeer:

Cookies & Local Storage

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login, security, and basic functionality
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: Track usage patterns to improve our platform
  • Marketing Cookies: Only placed with your explicit consent

Information Collected

  • Pages you visit and time spent on each page
  • Your IP address and unique device identifiers
  • Login details and authentication information
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Browser and device information

Your Control

Most browsers allow you to control cookie settings. However, please note:

  • We may not respond to "Do Not Track" signals in your browser
  • Disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to use Spopeer
  • You can manage cookie preferences in your account settings

7. User-Generated Content & Public Sharing

Exercise caution when sharing personal information in public areas of Spopeer. Once you post in public spaces:

  • Your content becomes visible to all other Spopeer users
  • Other users can collect, view, reproduce, and share your content
  • Search engines may index public content
  • We cannot control how others use content you share publicly

Recommendation: Only share information publicly that you're comfortable being seen by anyone. Use private messaging for sensitive conversations.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Under GDPR and similar laws, you have the following rights:

Right of Access

You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you. We'll provide this in a structured, commonly-used format.

Right to Rectification

You can correct inaccurate or incomplete information. You can edit your profile directly in your account settings.

Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten")

You can request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations. Once deleted, we cannot recover your account.

Right to Restrict Processing

You can limit how we use your information while we investigate disputes or verify data accuracy.

Right to Data Portability

You can download your personal data in a machine-readable format and transfer it to another service.

Right to Object

You can object to processing for marketing, analytics, or legitimate interests. We'll honor your choice.

Right to Withdraw Consent

For any processing based on your consent (e.g., marketing emails), you can withdraw consent anytime. Simply unsubscribe or update your preferences.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we've violated your rights, you can file a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., GDPR supervisory authority).

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:

  • 📧 Email: privacy@spopeer.com
  • 📬 Mail: Sports For All, Privacy Team, [Your Address]
  • 🔗 Account Settings: Manage preferences directly in your profile

We'll respond to your request within 30 days (or within the timeframe required by law).

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations:

Account Data

Retained while your account is active. After deletion, we retain data for:

  • Fraud Prevention: Up to 3 years
  • Legal Compliance: As required by applicable laws (typically 5-7 years)
  • Archival Purposes: In anonymized form for research and analytics

Marketing Communications

Retained until you unsubscribe. Unsubscribe requests are honored immediately.

Backup & Archival Data

We may retain copies of your data in secure backups for up to 90 days for disaster recovery.

User Right to Deletion

You can delete your account anytime. Upon deletion, we remove your personal data, except where we're legally obligated to retain it.

10. Data Security

Protecting your information is a top priority. We implement comprehensive security measures:

Technical Safeguards

  • 🔒 Encryption: All data transmitted via HTTPS/SSL encryption
  • 🔐 Password Security: Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • 🛡️ Firewalls & Intrusion Detection: Multi-layer network protection
  • 🔄 Regular Security Audits: Third-party penetration testing and vulnerability assessments

Administrative Safeguards

  • Limited employee access to personal data (need-to-know basis)
  • Employee confidentiality agreements and data protection training
  • Incident response procedures for data breaches

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a security breach, we will:

  • Notify affected users within 72 hours (or as required by law)
  • Inform relevant authorities and regulators
  • Provide information on mitigation steps and your rights

Note: No security system is 100% secure. While we implement strong protections, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children & Minors

Spopeer is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18 without verifiable parental consent.

For Users Under 18

  • You should use Spopeer under parental or guardian guidance
  • We recommend discussing online safety and privacy with your parents/guardians
  • Parents can request deletion of their child's account and data

For Parents/Guardians

If you discover that your child has created an account, you can:

  • Request account deletion and data removal
  • Contact us at privacy@spopeer.com with proof of parentage

12. International Data Transfers

Spopeer is a global platform. Your personal data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries other than your country of residence.

Data Protection

  • We only transfer data to countries with adequate data protection safeguards
  • We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) for transfers
  • We comply with GDPR and similar international privacy laws

Your Consent

By using Spopeer, you consent to the transfer of your personal data internationally as described in this policy.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Any material changes will be:

  • Posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date
  • Communicated to you via email or an in-app notification
  • Effective 30 days after notification (unless required sooner by law)

Your Continued Use of Spopeer signifies your acceptance of the updated privacy policy. If you disagree with changes, you have the right to delete your account.
