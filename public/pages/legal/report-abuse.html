Report Abuse

If you encounter abusive behavior, use in-app report and block controls first. For urgent issues, contact abuse@spopeer.com.

How to report in-app

  1. Open Safety Tools from Settings, Community, or Profile pages.
  2. Enter the offending user ID.
  3. Select a reason and optional notes.
  4. Submit report. Our moderation team reviews queue items continuously.

What happens next

Moderation actions can include warnings, temporary hides/removals, suspensions, and permanent enforcement for severe or repeated violations.
